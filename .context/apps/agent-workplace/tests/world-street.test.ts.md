## file

The street beyond the square is a place of its own in every pack. It is listed in Places, shown on the square's minimap as an arrow at the edge it leaves by, reached by walking out and left by walking back. It holds the unread Slack channels the square has no room for. Forty unread channels are more than the square and street hold together. The square's spots, less the two agents', hold the first channels; the street holds the next, one per spot. `AGENT_WORLD_SHOTS` saves `aw-alive-09-*.png`.
Why: a one-way street or an unbounded channel list would trap the avatar or overflow the place.
