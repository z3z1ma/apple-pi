Each pet is its own animal in its own place, drawn at its kind's height beside a standing character. Hover, left click, and the menu use its own verbs, poses, and Examine line. An asleep pet purrs when petted and wakes to play. None of this calls the backend. The avatar stands beside or behind a floor pet, never in front. `AGENT_WORLD_SHOTS` saves `aw-03-<theme>-<look>-*.png`. A forge bench read while standing there is the forge, not the pet.
Why: a pet action that reached the backend would look like a person action.
