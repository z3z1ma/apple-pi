## file

Characters use the committed built world UI with a fixture backend that records what the world asks. Each look shows its portrait. A restart of the same session keeps its look; a look the theme lacks falls back to the theme's first. Seated work alternates two frames at the same spot. Seen clears the badge. A newcomer appears in the doorway, fades in, passes the door's stand point, and walks to its spot. Stopping walks out the same way. A bark never covers another bark, the place title, or a panel. Behind a table, the table hides the avatar's legs and not its head. `AGENT_WORLD_SHOTS` saves `aw-05-*.png` and `aw-07-*.png`.
Why: a restart that changed the look, or a bark that covered the title, would make the same person unreadable.
