## file

Each pet has its own look, and no two places in a pack share one. Drawn height beside a standing character is about a third for a cat, half for a dog, less than a cat for a small animal or bird, and about a cat and a half for a perched parrot. At most one pet per pack is asleep. Smaller kinds are drawn smaller than that pack's cats and dogs.
Why: painted art size must not decide on-screen size, and a second sleeper would crowd the quiet pose.
