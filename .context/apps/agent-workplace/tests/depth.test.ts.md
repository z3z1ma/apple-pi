## file

A foreground piece covers someone whose feet are north of its `feet` line only on open floor behind its furniture or beside it, never in front of or around it. A body is wider than its feet, so a foot must be inside the piece's sides before the piece hides it. Furniture overhang can hide someone beside a footprint. Half-tile walk cells let a foot stand up to a quarter tile inside a traced edge. Open floor beside a tree trunk, under its canopy, is behind the canopy. The gallery's cut strips draw no foreground pieces. A fidget on furniture has its base on a footprint, and every piece in front of it is its surface. Hosts stand on open floor.
Why: covering floor in front of furniture would hide a person the scene should still show.
