## file

Each pack paints one outdoor place joined to the square: the fantasy harbour lane, the tech office shop street, and the anime school shopping street. The square has an exit to it and the street has a way back. `SQUARE_SPOTS_BEFORE` is the townsfolk-spot count painted before that place existed. Townsfolk and the ambient cast use the way back. An avatar arriving from the square can walk to the exit back.
Why: a one-way door would trap the avatar or hide the new place from the square.
