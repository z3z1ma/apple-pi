## symbol:placeWorkplace

Meeting presence is derived afresh from the normal home projection on each snapshot. Message sequence numbers are global, so the most recently updated open meeting wins when a person belongs to several. An unread escalation to the operator overrides that meeting; reading it restores the meeting or home. Only awake characters move. The directory, role, team counts, and sleeping roster remain authoritative and unchanged.

A task keeps its headquarters reachable even when its whole organization sleeps. An independent person may gain a board or a place to meet without becoming a manager.
