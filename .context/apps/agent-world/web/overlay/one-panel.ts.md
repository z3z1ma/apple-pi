## file

Every store that opens a panel over the world registers its `close` with `onePanel.closes` and calls `onePanel.opening` in each of its opening paths: `placePanels.open` (inn, rookery), `squarePanels.openCrier`/`openChannel`, `forge.open`/`openCard`/`openReview`, `atlassian.open` (quest office, library; `show` moves inside an open panel and does not), and `conversationPanel.open`. A new panel store must do both, or a panel opened over it leaves its state behind and it reappears when the other closes. `PlacesOverlay` renders every place's panels unconditionally and relies on this rule. Confirmations (ending a busy session, an outside write) are not panels and stay out of it.
