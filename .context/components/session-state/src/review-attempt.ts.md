This is an ordinary program-peer client owned by the runtime, not another model runner. Persist attempt, program, person and command identities before launch; the runtime derives a review scope from that stored program ID. The scope's generation, plan revision, owner, person and role must still match at launch. Ordinary Pi Exec program scopes retain their original behavior.

Save a terminal turn's structured output, observed usage and failure before rejecting cancellation or beginning cleanup. Keep the raw result distinct from validated output, and keep both distinct from completed cleanup. A malformed report can come from a completed model turn that incurred usage. A closed socket cannot prove disposal; scope disappearance under the existing guarded lifecycle does. Preserve partial results when cleanup fails.

Resolve current role/profile policy and compare its fingerprint with the planner catalog before either reuse lookup or new launch. A missing catalog entry also refuses execution. Per-attempt startup/completion checks alone allowed policy A during planning, B during a focused turn and restored A at job completion; B was recorded evidence, not an unobserved edit. Bind accepted components to their planned policy instead of adding a watcher. A resumed job resolves current role/profile policy before reuse lookup. Exact input identity includes that policy, the goal, bound context and output schema; callers additionally reject incomplete coverage. Reuse stays within the same job/plan, except the explicitly selected planner that produced the current plan is eligible across its own plan-revision increment. A new planner result advances plan revision, which prevents inspections under the old plan from receiving current coverage credit. Persist a validator's reuse block on the exact challenged attempt atomically with its validated output, before cleanup. A failed or interrupted retry must not erase the old challenge when current selections are cleared. The block points to its validating attempt; it does not rewrite the original report or claim cleanup completed. Return the actual reused or new attempt ID alongside its validated output so the job can record its current evidence explicitly. A new attempt gets new person/program/command identities. Retaining an old result is not permission for an obsolete attempt to update the current job. Exactly-once paid execution is not promised across lost results.

Role policy is recorded before launch and rechecked before dispatch and after the turn; a configuration change stops the attempt without erasing its raw result. The actual model/thinking observed at turn start is recorded separately. The normal role resolver chooses the mind and pool member. This client checks that the selected model belongs to the recorded policy instead of silently accepting a fallback; it does not select another model or advance profile-pool counters. Usage covers the observed correlated turn, not an invented total for every internal model the ordinary peer environment might run. The peer's native transcript remains its transcript owner.
