# Apple Pi wiki

Reusable LLM-oriented knowledge about apple-pi: design direction, synthesis, and context that outlives one task.

- `pages/` holds synthesized knowledge. Organize by real subject only.
- `raw/` holds retained source material when useful.
- `INDEX.md` is navigation. `LOG.md` is append-only history of knowledge changes.
- Link pages with `[[slug]]` or `[[slug#Heading|label]]`. A slug is the filename stem, unique across the wiki and matched case-insensitively.
- Repository `docs/`, tests, and `AGENTS.md` stay authoritative. Promote settled contracts there instead of keeping a competing copy here.
