# How exteriors are built

## Question

How are the exteriors, the connective tissue between places, built so they match the painted interiors and the vision of a cozy, fun world?

The tiled lane in the [headquarters prototype](../prototypes/headquarters/README.md) was rejected as far from the vision, while painted interiors were confirmed. Decide what an exterior is made of (painted scenes, painted set pieces over a ground, or something else), how large it is and how it joins the interiors, how Slack channels appear as characters in it, how it holds independent agents and the places' entrances, and the art pipeline. The operator judges the feel.
