# 29: Fidget objects

**What to build:** Every place in every theme has things to fidget with: pet the cat, ring the bell, light the candles. They are designed with the place, sit in its overlay slots, and never change work.

**Blocked by:** 15 The inn; 16 The rookery and mail; 21 The forge shows pull requests; 24 Atlassian sign-in and the quest office; 26 The library; 27 Slack sign-in and the town square
