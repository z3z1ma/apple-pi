# Organizational MRI and morphogenesis

## Intent

Reach a locked architecture decision for a self-optimizing organization of Pi sessions: an MRI that diagnoses where the organization wastes cognition and coordination, with evidence, and a morphogenesis loop in which managers change staffing, routing, authority, and topology and judge each change by the organization's fitness. The operator's brief is stored verbatim in `source.md`. The work runs as a wayfinder map (`map.md`, `decisions/`); each phase then gets its own spec.

Done when no unresolved in-scope decision ticket remains, Not yet specified is empty, and the map hands off to `/skill:to-spec`.

## Current State

Spec and eleven tickets approved by the operator on 2026-10-26, with four clarifications applied. Confirmed test seams: the workplace (faux sessions against a spawned runtime) and fitness without a live runtime (the evaluator over events, both adapters over a temporary database and hand-built scenarios).
