## Question

Does apple-pi model decision authority (who may resolve a bounded class of decisions) apart from the reporting manager and the task assignee, or is it enough to name a decision owner in a task or thread? Decide given that decision authority never widens tool permissions.

## Answer

Decided by the brief ([source.md](../source.md)); the operator directed on 2026-10-24 that the map follows it verbatim and that history data informs but does not gate. Reporting manager, task owner, and decision authority are three separate concepts that may coincide (section 5). A specialist can be the decision owner for an interface without managing anyone. Decision authority never widens tool permissions; those stay with roles and the runtime.

Physical form: the brief fixes the concept, not the machinery. The operator ruled on 2026-10-24 that the implementation is aggressively minimal and verification work is deferred; the form is settled in [Minimal physical form](18-minimal-physical-form.md).
