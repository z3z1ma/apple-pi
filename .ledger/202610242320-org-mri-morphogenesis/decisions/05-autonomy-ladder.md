## Question

The brief sets the principle (section 9, Layer C): reversible, low-risk operations gradually gain autonomy; structural or externally consequential changes stay under suitable authorization; the manager remains accountable. Decide the starting tier of each actuator (`assign`, `unassign`, `call_in`, `send_home`, `move`, `hire`, `let_go`, a new manager, a decision delegation, a `meet`), who approves each tier (the operator, the accountable manager, or nobody), and what evidence promotes an actuator to a more autonomous tier.

## Answer

The operator, 2026-10-24: managers make every organizational change on their own judgment (assign, unassign, call in, send home, move, hire, let go, a new manager, a decision delegation, a meeting) and then report it with the reasoning; the operator reads and corrects. There are no approval tiers. Tool permissions stay with roles and the runtime.
