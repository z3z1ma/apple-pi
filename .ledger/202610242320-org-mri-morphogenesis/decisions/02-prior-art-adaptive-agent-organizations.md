## Question

What does prior work say about adapting the structure of multi-agent LLM systems or human organizations to the work (topology search, dynamic team formation, org design under coordination cost, Conway's law, queueing at decision points)? Which findings transfer to lasting, stateful people, and which assume disposable agents?

## Answer

LLM topology search (GPTSwarm, AFlow, ADAS, DyLAN, AgentVerse, MaAS) optimizes per query with disposable agents and a benchmark judge; it does not value continuity or reorganization cost. What transfers to lasting people: Galbraith (reduce the need for coordination before adding capacity), Brooks (cold start of a hire), Reinertsen (measure queues and waiting, not utilization), Team Topologies (interaction modes: a specialist can advise without managing), and MAST as a failure vocabulary for diagnoses. No study compares lasting stateful agent organizations with disposable teams; apple-pi history is the evidence source.
