# How exteriors are built

## Question

How are the exteriors, the connective tissue between places, built so they match the painted interiors and the vision of a cozy, fun world?

The tiled lane in the [headquarters prototype](../prototypes/headquarters/README.md) was rejected as far from the vision, while painted interiors were confirmed. Decide what an exterior is made of (painted scenes, painted set pieces over a ground, or something else), how large it is and how it joins the interiors, how Slack channels appear as characters in it, how it holds independent agents and the places' entrances, and the art pipeline. The operator judges the feel.

Prototype (2026-10-18): round 4 of [prototypes/headquarters/](../prototypes/headquarters/README.md): a painted village square with doors to every function place, Slack channels as townsfolk, and overlaid water, lantern glows, and a cat; verdict below.

## Answer

Decided with the operator, 2026-10-18, from round 4 of the [headquarters prototype](../prototypes/headquarters/README.md): directionally right and a good experience compared with the tiled lane; much polish remains.

- **Exteriors are hand-painted scenes too,** made with the same pipeline and rules as interiors ([How places are built](15-how-places-are-built.md)): larger than the screen, walk grids and hotspots traced by hand, room left for sprites and overlays.
- **An exterior's painted building fronts carry one door for each function place,** and a door opens that place's interior.
- **What moves is overlaid,** with the painting left empty for it: water in a dry basin, glows on unlit lanterns, animals and things to fidget with.
- **Slack channels stand outdoors as townsfolk,** one character per channel, using the same sprites and portraits as agents; talking shows the channel's latest message and offers to reply as yourself or to ask an agent.
- **Polish noted:** the fountain water overlay looks janky (it needs drawn, animated water, not shapes); the unread badge over heads needs a better design (its number sits off centre).
