# Avatar, exteriors, and fidget objects

## Question

Who is the operator's avatar, how many exteriors does the first version have per theme, and what are fidget objects for?

## Answer

Decided with the operator, 2026-10-18.

- **Avatar:** each theme offers a few avatar looks; the operator picks one in Settings and it is kept. A theme pack supplies these looks (an amendment to [Theme pack contract](14-theme-pack-contract.md), which named one).
- **Exteriors:** one hand-designed exterior per theme in the first version (like the village square), holding every place's door; more exteriors come later.
- **Fidget objects:** pure play (pet the cat, ring the bell, light the candles); they never change work. Each place has its own, designed with the place.
