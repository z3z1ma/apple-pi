# Purpose and form of the world

## Question

What is Agent World for, moment to moment, and what form does it take?

Decide what the operator comes to the world to do (watch, decide, talk, review, steer the guild) and what form serves that: one town or separate areas, whether a coordinator's place is indoors, how indoor and outdoor areas differ, and what gives the world personality. Decide how places relate to tmux topology: whether places follow coordinators, tmux sessions, work, or groupings a theme chooses, which reopens the places in [What Agent World abstracts in tmux and Pi](05-world-ontology-and-truth.md). Account for dormant characters (decided 2026-10-18) and for the stewardship and embodied views. Earlier prototypes ([Guild Hall art and scale](04-guild-hall-art-and-scale.md), the town in [How places are built](15-how-places-are-built.md)) show what missed; the notch has no influence.

## Working notes

Operator, 2026-10-18:

- Core loop: the world is a home base open beside the operator's work all day (glance, go to a character, talk, answer); every place that calls for attention opens into the real action behind it.
- Form is open, with exteriors and interiors both available. The guild was always imagined as an interior with wings. Most interactions that matter probably happen in interiors; Slack channels might be characters in exteriors. The operator warned against arriving with too many assumptions: draw the form out of his picture rather than offering pre-cut shapes.
- Start: the operator starts in the **headquarters**, an interior whose form follows the theme (a guild in fantasy, an office in tech, a high-school classroom in anime). The coordinator appears there, as do most of the agents. The world is built up from such small pieces.
- Agents that report to the coordinator are in the headquarters. Agents that do not report to it can be scattered elsewhere in the world.
- Where an independent agent appears is a design choice, not a constraint: any heuristic that is logical and fun within the setting.
- Method: each function of the world (each system or kind of work it shows) gets its own place, mostly an interior, with its own look per theme. The rest of the world is connective tissue between those places, and that is where it is cozy and fun. Everything is hand-designed, not generated or laid out by rule.

## Answer

Decided with the operator, 2026-10-18 (details in the working notes above).

- **Purpose:** a home base open beside the operator's work all day: glance, go to a character, talk, answer. Every place that calls for attention opens into the real action behind it (review the PR, reply in the thread, plan with the coordinator).
- **Form:** every function of the world has its own hand-designed place, mostly an interior, drawn per theme; exteriors are the cozy connective tissue between those places. Nothing is generated or laid out by rule.
- **Start:** the operator starts in the headquarters, an interior. The coordinator and the agents that report to it are there; independent agents are elsewhere, placed by any heuristic that is logical and fun within the setting. Places follow functions and reporting, not tmux topology.
- **Starting set of places** (the operator called it "a perfect starting point"):

| Function | Fantasy | Tech office | Anime school |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Headquarters: the coordinator and its agents, in wings | Guild hall, guildmaster's desk, workbench wings | Office floor, lead's corner desk, team pods | Classroom, class rep at the teacher's desk, neighbouring classrooms as wings |
| Incoming work (Jira) | Quest board room with a clerk | War room with a kanban wall | Student council room with a request board |
| Code (GitHub: PRs, reviews, CI) | Forge: workbenches, a quenching trough for CI | Engineering lab with a CI status board | Science lab or tech club room |
| Knowledge (Confluence) | Library | Wiki lounge | School library |
| Talk with the outside (Slack channels as characters) | Town square, one townsperson per channel | Plaza café, one regular per channel | Schoolyard and rooftop, one student group per channel |
| Mail between agents | Rookery | Mailroom | Shoe lockers |
| Dormant agents | Inn rooms upstairs | Lounge with nap pods | Infirmary or dorm |
| Independent agents | Cottages and workshops along the lanes | Coworking spaces and studios | Club rooms |
| Connective tissue | Village lanes, bridge, market, pond | Campus paths, food truck, park | School grounds, sakura path, corner shop |

- Ambient life in the connective tissue reflects real events where it can (couriers carry real mail; characters walk between places when their work moves).
