# Human-process references and adaptation limits

Read on 2026-10-30. The source texts were fetched from the official sites and inspected directly after search. These notes support the design mechanisms; they do not establish benefits for AI agents.

| Primary source | Verified mechanism | Proposed use | What we are not importing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [GitLab: Directly Responsible Individuals](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/directly-responsible-individuals/) | One accountable owner consults relevant stakeholders; task ownership can differ from management; some decisions remain with another authorized person. | Distinguish accountable process owner, decision authority and consultation. | GitLab's claim that DRIs need not explain decisions, its approval thresholds, its RACI template or its internal governance. Our result retains rationale, evidence and uncertainty. |
| [Google SRE, Chapter 14: Managing Incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/) | Separate incident command, operations, communication and planning responsibilities; maintain current incident state; coordinate system changes; explicitly acknowledge command handoff. | Start with available authorized responders, add responsibilities as needed, keep updates and operational changes coherent, verify recovery. | Fixed declaration thresholds, human working-hour rotations, native subincident objects, production authority or a fixed number of responders. |
| [Liberating Structures: Troika Consulting](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/troika-consulting) | A problem owner presents a challenge; peers ask clarifying questions before advice; consultants discuss while the owner listens; the owner identifies useful takeaways. | Peer consultation that returns the decision to the owner after peers have engaged with each other's ideas. | Literal trios, chairs, camera behavior, fixed minute allocations or compulsory rotations through every participant's problem. |
| [Liberating Structures: What, So What, Now What](https://www.liberatingstructures.com/what-so-what-now-what) | Separate observations from interpretations and then from actions; individual reflection precedes group synthesis. | Retrospective and operating-review distinctions between facts, explanations, choices and follow-up. | Prescribed room layouts, group sizes, timings, diagrams or claims that the method has proved effectiveness in this agent system. |

## Source handling matters

The search synopsis was not the evidence. Its Troika timing details differed from the primary page, and its suggested next step of translating DRI ownership into a voting protocol did not follow the cited mechanism. The proposal relies on the directly inspected sequencing and authority distinctions, not those suggestions.

The Google SRE chapter carries CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licensing. The plan imports no source prose, diagrams, templates or assets into standing skills. During implementation, write original instructions, record provenance in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, and check terms before importing any protected expression. Concepts and proposed adaptations in this planning record are not a license determination.

The other candidate processes are proposals based on the repository's existing needs and capabilities. This research is a targeted mechanism check, not an exhaustive survey or a ranking backed by empirical agent-performance results.
