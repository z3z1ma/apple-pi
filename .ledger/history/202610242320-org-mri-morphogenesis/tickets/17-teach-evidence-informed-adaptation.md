# 17: Teach evidence-informed adaptation

Part of the approved spec [../spec.md](../spec.md), as redirected by [../direction.md](../direction.md) and the operator's sign-off notes of 2026-10-26.

**What to build:** People learn to use the instruments from their prompts and the docs, with no required ritual. The `<manager>` charter teaches that the organization reshapes itself whenever that is prudent, not only in sweeps: when the operator asks, while a task is being planned or implemented, when reports, escalations, or updates arrive, and when work looks constrained. At those moments: look at the organization (`org_waits`, `org_work`, `org_communication`, `org_expertise`, `org_fitness`); staff by context, asking `org_expertise` who knows an area before assigning or hiring; investigate causes through tasks, links, history, and mail; change the organization with the existing tools (assign, call in, move, hire, ask a subordinate manager to reorganize its subtree) when observed conditions justify it, or deliberately do nothing; use `org_fitness` and scenarios as one instrument among the others, when a dollar comparison helps, never as the objective to hill-climb; record important reasoning and revisit consequential decisions; prefer stability when change would cost more than it gives. It keeps fan-in as guidance ("when fan-in repeatedly constrains progress, consider whether changing authority, assignments, or reporting would help") in place of the current rule to hire a manager, and carries the brief's principles without fixed thresholds (reporting manager, task owner, and decision authority are separate roles that may coincide; loosely coupled work suits parallel people, tightly coupled work one strong owner, and risky work an independent challenger). Nothing requires a forecast or a fitness number before acting. Every person learns to record waits and results with `link`. The `<org>` section names the five inspection tools and `link`. `docs/organization.md` describes the tools, `docs/session-state.md` their sources, `docs/boundaries.md` says links are neither a hand-off record nor work records beyond tasks, and the README catalog matches.
