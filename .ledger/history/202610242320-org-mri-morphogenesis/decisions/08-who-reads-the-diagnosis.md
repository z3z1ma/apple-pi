## Question

Who reads diagnoses and through what surface? Candidates: managers in their sweep (tool or prompt section), the operator (`/org` command, tmux sidebar), Agent World, and `pi_exec` programs. Decide the first consumer and the one interface every surface shares.

## Answer

Decided by the brief ([source.md](../source.md)); the operator directed on 2026-10-24 that the map follows it verbatim and that history data informs but does not gate. One substrate interface serves every surface (section 9): `/org diagnose [--task]`, `pressures`, `propose`, `plan`, `apply`, `history` for the operator; a narrower diagnostic tool for managers; Agent World and tmux draw the same diagnoses. Expensive diagnosis runs when a pressure signal crosses a threshold, when a manager sweeps, or on request; no continuously running LLM (section 6).

Physical form: the brief fixes the concept, not the machinery. The operator ruled on 2026-10-24 that the implementation is aggressively minimal and verification work is deferred; the form is settled in [Minimal physical form](18-minimal-physical-form.md).
