## Question

Where do organizational episodes live and who learns from them? Decide whether they extend the replay lane's histories, become part of the person's record, or are derived on demand, and how a later regression is attributed to the episode that caused it.

## Answer

**Current answer:** no episode records. Learning is improving the estimate of fitness changes from `predicts` links compared with realized fitness, recomputed from the database ([Record of mutations and outcomes](23-record-of-mutations-and-outcomes.md)). The text below records how the answer evolved; later revisions win.

Decided by the brief ([source.md](../source.md)); the operator directed on 2026-10-24 that the map follows it verbatim and that history data informs but does not gate. An organizational episode is recorded whenever a meaningful outcome completes: problem characteristics, people and topology, coordination patterns, context costs, interventions, verification evidence, delivery time, and later regressions, attributed back to the episode (section 7). Not every message, not the transcript. Learning starts with explicit heuristics, recorded predictions, and retrospective comparison; a contextual policy selector comes only once evidence spans task classes. The ledger bundle and `history.json` are its natural anchor ([research 03](../research/03-reuse-replay-and-branch-search.md)).

Physical form: the brief fixes the concept, not the machinery. The operator ruled on 2026-10-24 that the implementation is aggressively minimal and verification work is deferred; the form is settled in [Minimal physical form](18-minimal-physical-form.md).

Revised by the thesis ([thesis.md](../thesis.md)), 2026-10-24: learning is improving the estimate of fitness changes from measured outcomes; see [Record of mutations and outcomes](23-record-of-mutations-and-outcomes.md).
