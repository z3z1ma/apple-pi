## Question

Is fitness the net value the organization realized over a past window, or the net value it is expected to produce next under its workload and horizon (which credits warm context, spare capacity, and good structure)? The thesis leans to expected future net value as the definition, with realized outcomes as the observations that train its estimate. Decide the definition, and how latent value is estimated without inventing health bonuses.

## Answer

The operator, 2026-10-24: fitness is defined as the expected net value over the horizon H under the expected incoming work W. The only ground truth is the realized net value over a trailing window, which is the same function evaluated on the work that actually arrived. Latent capacity (warm people at home) and fragility (knowledge held by one person) enter only through the expected W and the expected cost, with no health bonuses. Estimates of expected fitness improve from the gap between earlier predictions and what was later realized; that record is what keeps an expected value falsifiable.
