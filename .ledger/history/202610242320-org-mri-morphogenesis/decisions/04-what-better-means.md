## Question

What does a better organization mean here, and what is the first measured objective? Settle what counts as a verified outcome, which costs in `source.md` are measured first (the brief proposes avoidable coordination delay per verified outcome), which are hard constraints, and how operator attention is counted.

## Answer

Decided by the brief ([source.md](../source.md)); the operator directed on 2026-10-24 that the map follows it verbatim and that history data informs but does not gate. No single fitness score (section 2): minimise compute, critical-path delivery time, operator attention, coordination, context reconstruction, and reorganization cost, subject to hard constraints on correctness, verification, budget, permissions, and operational risk. The first measured objective is avoidable coordination delay per verified outcome (section 10); the outcome metric is verified, risk-adjusted value, not PR count, hires, or tasks closed (section 7). What counts as a verified outcome in apple-pi is deferred with all verification work: [What counts as a verified outcome](13-verified-outcome.md) is out of scope.

Physical form: the brief fixes the concept, not the machinery. The operator ruled on 2026-10-24 that the implementation is aggressively minimal and verification work is deferred; the form is settled in [Minimal physical form](18-minimal-physical-form.md).

Revised: the operator wants one rich fitness function for the organization and its subsets that drives all tuning; see [Org fitness function](19-org-fitness-function.md).
