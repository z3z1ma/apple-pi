# Retrospective

## Summary

The project-local LLM wiki direction was researched, shaped, specified, and completed. The operator directed closure as done.

## What Worked

Comparing the reference implementations established a small, Markdown-first design centered on `.wiki/`, lightweight provenance, and ordinary agent judgment.

## What Could Improve

Planning and verification became drastically disproportionate to a procedural skill. The process repeatedly turned plain engineering guidance into unnecessary evaluation machinery.

## Learnings

Procedural English for capable models should remain procedural English. Verification should be proportional to the production surface and actual risk rather than becoming a new system.

## Improvements

No additional process machinery was promoted. The durable improvement is to apply the existing proportionality rules directly and avoid inventing harnesses for simple packaged skills.
