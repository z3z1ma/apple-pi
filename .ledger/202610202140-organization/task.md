# Organization: Pi sessions as people with roles, souls, selves, and an org tree

## Intent

Turn apple-pi's peer sessions into an organization of lasting people. A person is a root Pi session with a stable id, a human name, a role it cannot shake (from a wide catalog that names each role's models exactly), a harness-drawn soul, a harness-kept self, a record, a mailbox, and a manager. The org is a tree rooted at the session the operator starts; managers hire from the catalog, build balanced teams with colliding perspectives, and bring in managers under them when their own attention would be the bottleneck. Mail and the directory are open across the org; the tree carries assignment, reporting, and escalation. The terminal is the org; Agent World is its picture. The subagent team shrinks to the two senses, explorer and researcher.
