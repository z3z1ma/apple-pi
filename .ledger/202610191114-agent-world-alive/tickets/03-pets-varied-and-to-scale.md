# 03: Pets varied and to scale

**What to build:** Fewer pets, and each one different. Each pack gets several distinct pet looks (cats of different coats, a dog, a bird, a rabbit, a hedgehog, chosen per theme), each with its own small animation set; a place has at most one pet, and no two places share the same pet look. Pets are drawn at their real size relative to the characters (a cat is about a third of a person's height), not scaled up.
