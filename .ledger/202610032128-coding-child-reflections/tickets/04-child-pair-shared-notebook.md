# 04: Share the primary notebook with coding-child pairs

**What to build:** Let the pair programmer attached to a coding child read shared learnings, follow exact notebook recall, and immediately add missed learnings through the primary-owned contribution path. Apply the same add-only authority to direct and nested child pairs without granting full shared-notebook maintenance. Include the relevant pair/runtime guidance, product-contract updates, and deterministic pair/session regression coverage alongside the confirmed SDK integration seam. Follow the [governing specification](../spec.md); a later TDD invocation still requires fresh seam confirmation. Preserve the primary pair's existing curation behavior and other pair responsibilities.

**Blocked by:** 01: Contribute and recall sourced child learnings.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] During an ongoing coding-child invocation, its pair can read a current primary-owned learning and use `revisit_note` to recover that learning's original sources. The same access works when the paired coding child is nested under another child.
- [ ] A coding child's pair can add a learning with host-validated original child evidence, and the accepted addition is visible in the primary notebook before the coding invocation finishes, rather than being staged until successful child completion.
- [ ] After successfully adding a learning, the child pair cannot supersede or retire shared conclusions or perform a shared retention sweep. These attempts leave existing conclusions intact while the primary and its pair retain their existing curation authority.
- [ ] A pair-originated learning and its original cited child evidence remain recallable from the primary after the ephemeral source child is disposed or its later invocation is cancelled. A late unaccepted pair submission after owner cancellation/navigation/shutdown cannot append into an unrelated session or branch.
