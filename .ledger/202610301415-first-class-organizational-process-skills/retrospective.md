# Planning retrospective

## What mattered

The repository already had skill packaging and substantial collaborative engineering procedures. The useful next layer was an outcome-oriented portfolio, not new workflow objects, another scheduler, or a skill for every communication technique.

One material choice required the operator: whether agents could select processes themselves. The operator chose discoverability within assigned work and existing authority. Existing explicit-entry engineering workflows remain unchanged.

The first proposal separated seven initial processes, five later possibilities, and three levels of evaluation. Pilar's cold read reconstructed the decision correctly and found no material comprehension blocker. That established teaching clarity for the reviewed version, not feasibility, autonomous selection or effectiveness.

The operator approved it but exposed a limitation: it treated today's role catalog too much as a boundary. The revision verified the two-manager fact, mapped all 32 roles, identified three missing management jobs, made specialist selection more concrete, and added three further process possibilities. The first seven-skill delivery set remains intact. No skills or roles were implemented during planning.

## Learnings

- Develop roles and processes in both directions. A needed responsibility may justify a new job; an overlooked specialist may expose an absent invitation or process. Neither finding means every role should participate in every effort.
- Verify actual capabilities and mandates. A VP/director title can still describe a read-only advisory role. Ownership, management authority, implementation and independent challenge need distinct accountable people when the work requires them.
- A draft's concentration around a few roles is a diagnostic lead, not measured runtime utilization. Investigate fit, discovery, specialization and overlap before changing staffing or deleting roles.
- Primary human-process sources supply mechanisms, not agent-effectiveness evidence. The search synopsis misstated details; directly read sources resolved the relevant sequencing. Fixed human group sizes and schedules are not a sensible default for agents.
- A comprehension check should exercise the actual misconception. The proposal's dependency diamond shows one release reached through two paths and counts it once. The prior reader has not been re-tested.

## Improvements carried into the plan

Each process has a trigger, authority boundary, accountable result and concrete live-exercise counterexample. Proposed management roles have continuing jobs and boundaries against existing advisors and managers. Evaluation covers discovery, package contents, selection/comprehension and actual controlled collaboration; it does not substitute a loader pass or scripted trace for useful behavior.

The implementation phase should preserve these distinctions, record which perspectives actually changed outcomes, and narrow or merge skills and roles that do not earn their place. This plan is ready for separately authorized implementation; delivery and live exercises remain unperformed.
