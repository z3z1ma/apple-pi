# Onboarding

Build enough working understanding for the agreed responsibility. Read and follow the [shared process conventions](../handbook-writing/references/processes.md).

The receiving manager owns readiness, an experienced colleague supplies context, and the newcomer demonstrates understanding. Use `technical-writer` to repair stale guidance and `developer-experience-lead` when the entry path is the obstacle.

1. **Bound readiness.** Name what the person needs to do and what remains outside their role. Point to current goals, authoritative sources, relevant people and conversations. Native startup notices establish identity and references, not comprehension.
2. **Orient through use.** Choose a representative task or read-only demonstration, not an exhaustive history dump. The newcomer follows the entry path, asks questions and explains the system in their own words. A document link does not establish access or a working setup.
3. **Check and repair.** Observe the smallest meaningful result. Fix misleading shared guidance at its owner, separate missing knowledge from missing authority, and retain any readiness gaps. Use [handoff](../handoff/SKILL.md) if responsibility is actually moving.

Done is demonstrated readiness for the stated scope, or an explicit remaining gap with an owner. A person's standing identity needs no invented productive assignment; practice is labeled as practice, not delivered business value.
